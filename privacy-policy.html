AediaX Calculator is designed to perform mathematical calculations locally on your device. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including but not limited to:

All calculations are performed locally on your device. We do not receive or store your calculation history.

Advertising

Analytics

The current version of AediaX Calculator does not use analytics services to collect user information.

Data Security

Since the application does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

Children's Privacy

AediaX Calculator is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
