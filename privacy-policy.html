Privacy Policy

Effective: July 22, 2026

AediaX Tech Private Limited ("AediaX", "we", "our", or "us") operates the AediaX Calculator mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use our app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information We Collect

AediaX Calculator is designed to perform all mathematical operations directly on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Contact lists, location data, or media files
  • Documents, messages, or payment credentials
  • Device identifiers or network information

Calculator Data

All calculations are processed locally on your device. We do not receive, store, or have access to your calculation history or results.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions essential for its core functionality. Should future versions require additional permissions, they will be explicitly requested through the Android system, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

Advertising

The current version of AediaX Calculator does not display any third-party advertisements. If we introduce ads in later releases, we will revise this policy to clearly disclose any data processed by advertising partners.

Analytics

We do not employ analytics services or tracking tools that collect user information. Your usage remains entirely private.

Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data, no information is stored on our servers. All data stays on your device, under your control.

Children's Privacy

AediaX Calculator is appropriate for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
